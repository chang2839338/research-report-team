# Report Quality Rubric

Use this rubric before final delivery.

Score each item 0-2:
- 0: missing or weak
- 1: acceptable but needs improvement
- 2: strong

## Criteria

1. Goal fit
   - The report answers the user's decision or reporting purpose.

2. Audience fit
   - Detail level, tone, language, and structure match the target reader.

3. Evidence quality
   - Important factual claims are backed by relevant sources or clearly marked as assumptions.

4. Source traceability
   - Sources are linked or identified clearly enough to inspect.

5. Analytical clarity
   - Options, criteria, tradeoffs, and reasoning are explicit.

6. Recommendation quality
   - The recommendation follows from the evidence and acknowledges uncertainty.

7. Risk coverage
   - Material risks, constraints, and dependencies are visible.

8. Usability
   - The final document is easy to act on.

## Gate

Final delivery should usually score at least 13 out of 16, with no zero on goal fit, evidence quality, analytical clarity, or recommendation quality.

If the score is lower, revise the specific weak sections before responding.
