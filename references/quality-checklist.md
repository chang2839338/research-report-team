# Report Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before treating a report as final.

## Goal Fit

- [ ] The report directly answers the user's decision or reporting goal.
- [ ] The target reader is clear.
- [ ] The output format matches the request.

## Evidence

- [ ] Current factual claims include source links or are marked as assumptions.
- [ ] Facts and interpretation are separated.
- [ ] Source limitations or uncertainty are visible.

## Analysis

- [ ] Decision criteria are explicit.
- [ ] Options are compared against the same criteria.
- [ ] The recommendation follows from the evidence.
- [ ] Major tradeoffs are visible.

## Risk

- [ ] Important risks are included.
- [ ] Caveats and assumptions are not hidden.
- [ ] The report explains when the recommendation should be revisited.

## Usability

- [ ] The executive summary is short and useful.
- [ ] The next actions are concrete.
- [ ] The report can be used in a real meeting or planning session.

## Quick Approval Rule

Approve the report only if:

- It answers the goal.
- It has enough evidence for its claims.
- It gives a clear recommendation or decision framing.
- It shows meaningful risks.
- It tells the user what to do next.
